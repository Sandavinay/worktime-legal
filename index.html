Work Time Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026
App: Work Time (Android package com.sandavin.work_time)
Operator: Sandavin
Contact: worktimehelpline@gmail.com · WhatsApp +91 9177840108

This policy applies specifically to the Work Time mobile and web application used by companies to record attendance, manage employees, and run payroll. It explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Work Time is a workplace tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

1. Who this policy covers

Work Time is used by:

  • Company administrators who create a company account
  • Employees who punch attendance on their phone
  • Shared workplace punch devices set up by an administrator

The company that invites an employee is the organisation using that employee’s workplace data. We process that data to provide the service the company requested.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how the app is used, we may collect:

  • Account and identity. Mobile number, password (stored as authentication credentials, not as readable text), optional email, company name, employee name, and other profile fields the admin enters (such as date of join or date of birth).
  • Attendance. Punch in and punch out times, workplace or onsite mode, corrections made by an admin, week offs, holidays, and related attendance status.
  • Precise location. GPS coordinates and a location name when an admin signs in (required to record the login) and when an employee or punch device records a punch that uses GPS. We may reverse-geocode coordinates to a place name.
  • Photos and face data. Camera images captured for face enrolment and for punch verification. These images are used to confirm that the enrolled employee is punching. They are not used as a public profile picture unless the admin stores a separate employee photo.
  • Device and login data. Approximate platform (for example Android, iOS, or web), login date and time, login location, and session activity so administrators can review sign-ins.
  • Workplace settings. Office and onsite locations, geofence radius, punch rules, overtime and leave settings, and assigned punch devices.
  • Payroll. Salary figures, allowances, deductions, and payslip data entered by the company admin.
  • Billing. Plan selection, employee limits, payment amount, payment status, and payment identifiers from our payment processor.
  • Support. Messages you send to customer care by email or WhatsApp.

The app requests camera, location, internet, and vibration permissions only to provide these features. If you refuse a permission, the related feature will not work (for example, GPS punch or admin login that requires location).

3. How we use the information

  • Create and secure company, employee, and punch-device accounts
  • Send one-time passwords to a mobile number
  • Record, display, and correct attendance
  • Verify punches with face and/or GPS according to company settings
  • Show salary and payslips when the company allows it
  • Bill the company for the attendance service
  • Show administrators a history of their logins
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorised access
  • Respond to support requests and meet legal duties

We do not sell personal information. We do not use face images for advertising.

4. How we share information

We share data only as needed to run Work Time:

  • The employer / company admin. Employee attendance, profile, punch photos where stored, location of punches, and salary records for that company. One company cannot read another company’s data.
  • Supabase. Hosting for authentication, database, and file storage.
  • MSG91. Delivery of SMS one-time passwords to mobile numbers.
  • Face matching provider (including Luxand where configured). Face templates or images needed to enrol and recognise an employee for attendance.
  • Razorpay. Processing of plan payments and checkout.
  • Map and geocoding services (including OpenStreetMap / Nominatim). Turning coordinates into a location name or showing a map pin.
  • Google Play and device platforms. App distribution, crash and device signals they collect under their own policies.
  • Law and safety. If required by law, or to protect users, the service, or others.

Service providers are allowed to use this information only to provide their service to us, subject to their contracts and applicable law.

5. Location, camera, and biometric-style data

Precise location is collected when the company enables GPS punch or when an administrator signs in. Location is used for attendance proof and login records, not for ads or independent tracking of people outside those flows.

Camera access is used only to capture a live face photo for enrolment or punch. Face data is sensitive. It is processed to verify attendance for the employee’s company. Admins can delete an employee, which removes that employee’s stored face files and related face-provider records from our service as part of deletion.

6. Retention

We keep account, attendance, payroll, billing, and login records while the company account is active and as needed for payroll, disputes, security, and legal retention. If a company or employee account is deleted, we delete or de-identify associated personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must keep it for law, billing, or security.

7. Security

Access to company data requires a signed-in account. Passwords are handled by the authentication service. Face and photo files are stored in restricted storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. You should use a strong password and sign out on shared devices.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Administrators can update company profile details and employee records in the app.
  • Employees can view their own attendance and, when enabled, salary.
  • You can turn off camera or location permission in device settings; some features will then be unavailable.
  • You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data by asking your company admin or by emailing worktimehelpline@gmail.com.
  • If you are in a region with additional privacy rights, we will honour valid requests as required by law.

9. International processing

Work Time is offered from India. Servers and processors may be located in India or other countries. If you use the app, your information may be processed outside your state or country where data-protection rules can differ.

10. Children

Work Time is a workplace product. It is not intended for children. If we learn that we collected data from a child, we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. The effective date at the top will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy. Material changes will be reflected on this page and in the app.
